# Arrify 0.3.0 (42) — audiobooks beta

- Optional Audiobooks section with your bookshelf, chapters, author and narrator details.
- One Arrify sign-in: the server manages each listener’s private Audiobookshelf account.
- Continue unfinished books from Home, and find books alongside music in Search.
- Native background playback, Lock Screen controls, and AirPlay.
- Download complete books for offline listening, with visible progress, pause/resume, and removal controls.
- Adjust narration speed and skip intervals, save bookmarks, and use a sleep timer or stop at the end of a chapter.
- Keep whole books in Up Next. Continuing automatically to the next queued book is optional and off by default.
- Preserve offline listening positions across reopening, synchronize them with Audiobookshelf, and resolve conflicting positions explicitly.
- Keep music’s cached playback transitions fast when switching back from a book.

Please test books on iPhone and iPad: download and reopen offline, rewind and resume,
change speed, use chapters/bookmarks/sleep timers, and move between devices.
Physical-device testing is still needed for hardware AirPlay, audio interruptions,
and long sessions while locked. Dedicated CarPlay browsing awaits an entitlement.
