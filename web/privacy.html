Arrify Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Arrify is a personal music player that connects to an Arrify server you choose. This policy describes the iOS app and the Robnets-operated Arrify and review-demo servers. Independently operated servers are controlled by their operators; ask that operator about their practices before connecting.

Information used

The app sends your login credentials to your selected server over HTTPS. That server stores account information and a password hash, and issues session credentials. The app stores session credentials in the device Keychain and keeps connection, device-identifier, and playback preferences locally.

Your server processes library searches, track identifiers, playback position, queue state, and device/session information to browse music, stream audio, resume playback, and coordinate listening. Server administrators can see active listening sessions. Web account features may also store an email address, profile, playlists, and listening or search history. Network servers and reverse proxies may record IP addresses, request times, and technical errors for operation and security.

Audio, artwork, and services

Music and artwork are fetched from your selected server. AirPlay sends playback and metadata to the receiver you select. The native app does not record microphone audio or access your contacts or photo library. Optional web integrations, if enabled by a server operator, also involve the relevant service's privacy practices.

Optional Lock Screen status

If you choose to follow playback on another device, Arrify sends the song title, artist, output name, and playback status through Apple Push Notification service to your Live Activity. Your server stores an activity token linked to your signed-in session for up to seven hours. Remote controls require verification that the playing device is reachable on the same local network, and the app registers them only while connected to Wi-Fi. Stop it in Arrify or sign out to end it.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to authenticate users, deliver requested playback, maintain the service, and troubleshoot problems. Arrify does not include advertising trackers, sell personal information, or use it for cross-app advertising. Hosting and network providers process traffic needed to operate the service. Apple separately processes TestFlight installation, crash, and feedback information under Apple's privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

In the iOS app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. Enter your current password and confirm permanent deletion. Accounts awaiting approval or frozen accounts can use Delete an existing account on the sign-in screen. No administrator approval, email, or support request is needed to delete an account.

Deletion removes your account, profile and avatar, liked songs, personal and shared playlists, listening and search history, request records, hosted parties and guest content, preferences, and linked-service credentials from the active server. All web and app sessions, refresh tokens, playback grants, and remote Lock Screen registrations are revoked. The app stops playback and clears its credentials, artwork and audio caches, and diagnostic files on the deleting device. Shared server music and other users’ accounts are not deleted. Copies already exported to other devices or third-party services cannot be remotely erased by Arrify.

The server retains only non-identifying reserved numeric IDs and whether initial server setup was completed, to prevent ID reuse and the restoration of deleted accounts from old databases. Robnets-operated database backups and legacy acquisition requester references are scrubbed by an hourly erasure job, normally within one hour and within 24 hours of deletion. Other server operators control their backups and must run the same erasure process and preserve the deletion ledger when restoring data. Infrastructure access logs may retain IP addresses and request times under the hosting provider’s retention policy; they do not contain account passwords or deletion request bodies.

Security and choices

HTTPS protects network traffic and the Keychain protects stored app credentials. Only connect to servers you trust. The app supports account creation; administrators approve new accounts before listening is enabled. Signing out revokes the current app session but does not delete the account. Arrify does not sell subscriptions or use Sign in with Apple; deleting Arrify does not cancel a separate Apple Music or Spotify subscription.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact your server administrator through the private channel used to arrange your account, or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight. Do not post personal account information in public GitHub issues. Updates will appear here with a revised effective date.
